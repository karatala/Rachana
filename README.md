# Rachana

Write music, shape performances, explore arrangements and learn your parts. Work with notation, tablature and Piano Roll in one project, with your own sounds and SF2 support.

**[Download the app](https://github.com/karatala/Rachana/releases)**

> [!WARNING]
> **Alpha version.** Use at your own risk. Projects may become corrupted. Keep backups of important work.

## Write and arrange

- **Notation, tablature and Piano Roll together.** Edit the same part in the view that suits you. Change note pitch, timing, duration and velocity, and edit some MIDI events and notation articulations.
- **Keep the original performance.** MIDI import preserves exact note timing and velocity. Notation does not force your performance onto a grid.
- **A library of musical ideas.** Store phrases independently of their place in the composition. Use linked copies: editing the original idea updates every linked instance.
- **Arrangement variants.** Try different song structures, tempos and instruments within one project. Listen to each version, then apply your chosen arrangement to the score.
- **Audio and automation.** Add and trim audio clips, set fades, and draw automation curves.

## Play and learn

- **Learn your parts.** Follow colored notes in the score and on customizable virtual keyboards, fretboards and drum kits. Keyboard color themes include support for common color-coded piano learning stickers.
- **Your own sounds.** Open SF2 banks, choose the instruments you need and save them as compact standalone files without data loss. Compare sounds while your composition plays.
- **Help with notation basics.** Most notation terms and palette controls have explanations. Right-click on desktop or press and hold on mobile to read them.

## Make it yours

- **Quick access to your tools.** Keep sounds, notation tools, metronome tempos and loop presets in your favorites. Assign custom shortcuts to almost any action, or find it through command search.
- **File exchange.** Open Guitar Pro, MusicXML and MIDI files. Export MIDI and share projects with the custom instruments they use.
- **Work across devices.** Use a native app or your browser on almost any device. Work offline after the first complete app download, or install the PWA on your home screen or desktop. A portable Windows version is also available.
- **Pick up where you left off.** Projects save and reopen automatically so you can continue your work.

> [!NOTE]
> **Current limitation:** Undo and redo history is lost when the app closes and is not restored when a project automatically reopens.
